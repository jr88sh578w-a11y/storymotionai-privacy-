StoryMotionAI Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

StoryMotionAI ("we", "us") is an app that turns your photos and text descriptions into short AI-generated videos. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who helps us process it, and the choices you have. If you have any questions, contact us at storymotionai.support@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account information. Your email address and a password when you create an account. Your password is stored securely by our account provider and is never visible to us.
  • Photos you choose. When you use Photo to Video, the photo you select is uploaded so the video can be created. We only access photos you pick yourself.
  • Text you enter. The descriptions (prompts) you type for Text to Video and Photo to Video.
  • Generated videos. The videos created from your photos and prompts.
  • Credits and purchases. Your credit balance and a record of your purchases, including the transaction details Apple provides to confirm a purchase. We never see or store your payment card details; payments are handled entirely by Apple.
  • Basic technical information. Standard logs, such as error reports, that our service providers create when the app contacts our servers.

We do not use advertising, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your personal information.

How we use your information

  • To create your account and sign you in
  • To generate the videos you request
  • To manage your credits and confirm your purchases
  • To keep the app secure, prevent misuse, and fix problems
  • To respond when you contact us

Who processes your information

We use trusted service providers to run the app. They handle your information only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase provides our account system, database, and storage for photos you upload.
  • Replicate runs the AI models that create your videos. Your photo (as a link to the stored image) and your text prompt are sent to Replicate to generate the video, and Replicate handles that data under its own privacy policy.
  • Apple processes in-app purchases and provides us with confirmation that a purchase was completed.

We do not share your information with anyone else except when required by law.

Your photos

Please only upload photos that you own or have permission to use. Do not upload photos of other people without their consent. Your uploaded photos are stored under your account with a unique, hard-to-guess link so that the video service can access them. Photos are removed when you delete your account.

AI-generated content

Videos are created automatically by artificial intelligence, so results may be imperfect or unexpected. You are responsible for how you use the videos you create. Do not use the app to make content that is illegal, harmful, hateful, or sexual, or that impersonates or harms others. We may block requests and remove accounts that misuse the app.

How long we keep your information

We keep your account information, credits, and purchase records for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time inside the app. When you do, we permanently delete your account, your remaining credits, your purchase history, and your uploaded photos. Videos you saved to your own Photos library stay on your device, because they belong to you.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the app under Account, then Delete Account.
  • Ask for a copy, correction, or deletion of your information by emailing storymotionai.support@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.
  • Photos permission. The app asks permission to save videos to your Photos library. You can change this at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws, such as the right to access or delete your data. Contact us and we will help.

Children

StoryMotionAI is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and server-side checks on credits and purchases. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date above. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the new version.
